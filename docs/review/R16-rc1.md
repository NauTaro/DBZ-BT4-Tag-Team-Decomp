# R16-rc1 review

## Scope

This is a preview, not a claim that all reported bugs are fixed.

Implemented: Default Attacks only; actor-relative cinematic midpoints; grab
cut cancellation; removal of ordinary-projectile camera transfer; resource
reloads for transformations in extra actor slots; independent WinMM voice
buffers; empty/default device preferences; persistent executable/window icon.

## Recorded checks

- 18 native transformation steps, including actors 8 and 9 in a ten-fighter match.
- Camera geometry/grab audit: 5 checks. Camera policy: 31. Shared preparation: 12.
- Local result actions: rematch, character select and main menu.
- Final executable: two native clients joined an Internet Tag Team room by code,
  fought, reached results and rematched.
- Final executable: direct and matchmaking 1v1 sessions with rollback, simulated
  35 ms latency and 3% packet loss; 6,014 common ticks, 2,872 in combat, without
  a recorded mismatch in the accepted runs.
- Voice: direct transport, Worker relay and proximity attenuation using synthetic
  audio. Far-distance measured RMS was zero. Physical microphone muted, physical
  output and unavailable-device checks were also performed. A 180-second voice
  toggle stress run ended without a crash. Physical microphone speech was not
  recorded or transmitted in these checks.
- All 20 embedded icon images matched the supplied ICO; installed core hashes
  matched the tested preview.

## Open issues and limits

The multicolored rectangles reported for Hit's first rush remain unresolved.
Fresh-process visual checks, additional actor slots, simultaneous techniques
and alternate stages did not reproduce them. A GPU/GPU Internet test of Hit's
first rush reached correct victim damage and recovery. That test preceded the
final identity/protocol-only rebuild.

Mixed GPU/no-render test clients diverged in the artificial Hit fixture; this
did not recur with GPU on both clients. The mixed runs are not acceptance tests
for ordinary online gameplay. OpenGL produced no usable captures with that
capture mechanism, so its visual appearance was not verified.

The exact old SDL access violation was not reproduced. A changed audio backend
and passing stress checks are not proof against every crash. The tests use one
Windows PC, selected devices, fighters, moves and stages. Cloudflare Free quotas
apply. No universal frame-rate claim is made.
